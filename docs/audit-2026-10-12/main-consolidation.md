engine and platform layout. Numerous leaves are probes, WIP checkpoints, rejected
experiments or HOLD performance candidates. File differences and graph divergence
alone cannot establish a missing feature. The source receipts identify recovered
contracts, current successors, rejected behavior and unresolved implementation.
Author intent is marked unknown where no handoff or recorded decision exists.

The history merge explicitly resolves files to the current architecture plus
these reviewed ports. Old source and unfinished snapshots remain recoverable in
main ancestry and `refs/kontra-recovery/20261012/`; old prototypes do not become
active merely by adding their commits as parents. Remaining v1 bank ownership,

fields and named effect/UI laws are listed in the receipt. Native Kontakt/Falcon
behavior remains the acceptance reference; this consolidation does not certify
