Initial-state CPU UI witnesses for Contrabass Clarinet, Bassoon and Flute found
all requested resources: respectively 21/21, 21/21 and 22/22; no missing,
ambiguous, corrupt or read-failed resources. This covers requested assets on
rendered states, not every hidden page, gesture or native pixel equivalence.
The old `Menu.hierarchical` warning was absent in this current audit.

Separate real V2Loader probes used an unseeded UVI owner, explicit empty
controller input, a held velocity-96 note and 96,000 output frames. Owner
acknowledgements and ordered command drain completed; sample status was settled.
Bassoon at key 60, Flute at key 60 and Contrabass Clarinet at key 48 each produced
96,000 nonzero frames (maximum 2, 6 and 2 voices). No runtime faults, stream
underruns or capacity drops were observed in these short windows. Contrabass
Clarinet key 60 produced no voices; the successful key-48 trial prevents treating
that single no-voice result as a loader failure. Native equivalence, all gestures,
all articulations and the other 37 programs' audio remain unverified.
